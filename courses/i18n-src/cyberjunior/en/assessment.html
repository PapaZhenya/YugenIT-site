Lab report template (mandatory submission format)

  1. Objective — what was done and why.
  2. Environment and tools — where it was performed (lab, platform, software).
  3. Procedure — step-by-step actions.
  4. Findings — what was discovered, with evidence (screenshots/logs).
  5. Risk assessment — severity of each finding (low/medium/high).
  6. Recommendations — how to remediate/protect against it.
  7. Conclusion — brief summary and what was learned.
  8. Ethics confirmation — work was carried out only in an authorized environment.
