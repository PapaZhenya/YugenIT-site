Program map

The program runs for 12 weeks. Five blocks, each building on the previous one.

BlockWeeksFocusBlock outcome
1. Foundation1–3Networks, OS, threat modelUnderstands how systems work and where their weaknesses are
2. Attack4–6Reconnaissance, vulnerabilities, exploitationFinds and exploits common vulnerabilities in the lab
3. Defense7–8Hardening, crypto, monitoringConfigures basic protection and detection
4. Response9–10IR, forensics, ATT&CKInvestigates an incident from logs, knows Blue Team basics
5. Specialization11–12Career, capstone projectBuilds a portfolio, completes the final CTF/audit
