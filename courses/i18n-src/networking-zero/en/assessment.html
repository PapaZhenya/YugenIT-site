Final project (modules 8-9)

Build a network in Packet Tracer made up of two subnets connected by a router, with several workstations and a nominal "outside" connection. Configure addressing, get connectivity working between the subnets (ping), then find and fix one deliberately introduced fault. Report: diagram, configuration, how you tested it, what you fixed and why.
