A full list of free tools, training platforms, and courses you'll need to complete the course and continue developing afterward.

Tools (free)

  • Cisco Packet Tracer — a simulator: build networks out of routers and switches without any hardware. Parts II-III.
  • Wireshark — a traffic analyzer: look at real packets. Modules 1-2 and 6.
  • System commands — ping, tracert/traceroute, ipconfig/ip, nslookup.

External training platforms and courses (linked, free)

  • Cisco Networking Academy / SkillsForAll — free courses on networking and on Packet Tracer itself.
  • Professor Messer (Network+) — free videos for the CompTIA Network+ certification.

Where to go after the course

  • CompTIA Network+ — an entry-level networking certification.
  • Cisco CCNA — deeper, and valued by employers.
  • Next — network security (see the cybersecurity course on the platform).
